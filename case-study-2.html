Project overview

This case study compares Cyclistic casual riders and annual members using trip data from January through May 2025. The analysis was performed with SQL in Google BigQuery, with charts presented using Chart.js. Trip patterns may suggest marketing opportunities, but they do not directly reveal each rider's purpose.

Scope: five months of trip records. Counts represent trips, not unique riders.

Key takeaways

  • Annual members generated approximately 70.6% of trips in the analysis dataset.
  • Casual riders had a longer average ride duration (21.52 minutes) than members (11.25 minutes).
  • Casual trip volume was highest on Saturday; member trip volume was highest on Thursday.
  • Both groups recorded their highest hourly trip volume at 5 PM. Members also showed strong activity during the morning.
  • Trip volume rose from January to May in this dataset; additional data would be needed to confirm seasonality and explain the increase.

Suggested business actions

Cyclistic could test membership promotions for casual riders on weekends and near popular casual-rider stations. Campaigns should be evaluated using membership conversion rates and acquisition costs before expanding. The trip data alone cannot confirm riders' motivations or the impact of any campaign.
